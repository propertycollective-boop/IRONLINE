Certifications & Compliance

Credentialed to do the work, not just to bid on it.

Every certification this work requires is already in place inside our operations, and is being carried directly into the Ironline entity as the business is formally established.

CRED / BOILER CODE

National Board “R” Certificate

Authorization to repair and alter boilers and pressure-retaining items under a documented, NBIC-compliant quality system.

CRED / FABRICATION

ASME Code Certification

Covers fabrication and assembly of new pressure-part and code items manufactured in-house.

CRED / PUBLIC WORK

Public Works Contractor Registration

Registered in New Jersey, our home state, with additional state registrations pursued as public-sector work requires — every state gates this separately, so we register where the work is.

CRED / CLASSIFICATION

Trade-category and maximum-workload classification for public building projects, starting with New Jersey's DPMC and extending state by state as we bid there.

CRED / TRADE LICENSES

Electrical, plumbing, HVAC, and environmental licensing as required by jurisdiction.

CRED / SAFETY

Verified incident rates, written safety programs, and training records through ISNetworld and Avetta.

CRED / SURETY

Bonding & Insurance

Bid, performance, and payment bonds, plus general liability, umbrella, and pollution coverage where scope requires.

CRED / FEDERAL

Federal entity registration for eligibility on federal contracting opportunities.

Safety

Safety is a condition of work — never a variable in the schedule.

Working inside another owner's plant means giving up home-field familiarity. We accept a job only when the hazards are understood, the controls are staffed, and any worker on site can stop the work.

Universal stop-work authorityEvery worker on every job can stop work for a safety concern — no exceptions, no retaliation, documented restart.
A named supervisor and safety lead, every jobOne Ironline supervisor and one dedicated site safety lead are assigned before mobilization — never left to a rotating crew.
Hazards reviewed at bid timeEnergy isolation, confined space, hot work, and lifting hazards get planned and priced before the estimate is submitted, not discovered in the field.
High-hazard work earns its way inConfined-space entry, boiler tube work, and heavy lifts are sequenced behind proven, lower-hazard jobs with our most experienced crews.
Code-aligned boiler workBoiler and pressure-part repair follows a documented quality system built to National Board (R Certificate) standards.
How We Engage

From first call to closeout.

A straightforward path from a site conversation to a completed job, with the same review gates we'd want a contractor to use on our own plant.

I.

Discovery & Site Assessment

We walk the site, review the outage calendar and installed equipment, and understand the problem before proposing scope.

II.

Scope & Estimate

Labor, equipment, materials, and risk are built into a reviewed estimate — priced from real cost, not a rough number.

III.

Award & Mobilization

Contract terms, insurance, and staffing are confirmed, and a named supervisor and safety lead are assigned before crews arrive.

IV.

Execution & Reporting

Daily hours and progress are tracked against plan, with changes documented and communicated as they happen, not at closeout.

V.

Closeout & Lessons Learned

Final documentation, a completed punch list, and a debrief that feeds directly into how we plan your next outage.
